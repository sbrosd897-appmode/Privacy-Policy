Privacy Policy – Num Run

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Num Rum ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you play the game. The app is provided by S Bros.

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store your personal data.

Your game data (levels completed, stars, coins, items, settings, scores and achievements) is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, operating system, app version) and approximate location derived from your IP address. Google may use this data to show you ads that are relevant to you.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). To learn how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Other Google Play services

The app may use Google Play features such as in-app updates and in-app reviews. These are provided and controlled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing

When you press a share button, the app opens your phone's standard share menu so that you can send a message or image to the app you choose. We do not receive that content.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at the e-mail below.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because game data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. For data collected by Google through ads, please use your Google account's ad settings.

7. Security

We do not collect personal data ourselves, which limits the risk. Google applies its own security measures to the data it processes.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the "Last updated" date will change.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at sbrosd897@gmail.com.
